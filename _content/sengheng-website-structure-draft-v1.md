- Supply of Marine Class Vessels & Boats

- Ship Chandler Services  
  We provide supply services of provisions, cabin stores, deck and engine stores, safety and technical equipment, bonded stores, technical services, fuel oils & fresh water for offshore vessels, logistics and support thru out Sarawak & Sabah, Malaysia.

## 4.2 Shipping & Forwarding
- Custom & Immigration Clearance  
  Malaysia Royal Custom authorized forwarding agent for custom clearance service provide to our clients. We do provide services such as inward & outward clearance for vessels, crewing arrangement, crew's work permit application, crew sign on/off, vessel berthing arrangement, application & renewal for seafarers.

- Air/ Sea Freight  
  We provide air & sea freight arrangement for our clients ship spare, equipment, and others.

- Inland Transport  
  We provide crews transportation, lorry crane, mobile crane, trailer, and others for our clients.

- Warehousing  
  We offer 30,000 sqft and above of warehousing space in Miri, Sarawak for our clients to storage there offshore equipment. Our warehouses are monitored by CCTV and manned 24 hours a day by security guards.

- Packing & Moving  
  We offer packing & moving services for our clients for there equipment or general cargos together with freight arrangement in ONE-STOP station.

- Tug & Barge Service  
  We owned and operate fleets of tug boats and barges providing cost efficient sea transportation services for general cargo, containers, machinery, construction material, heavy equipment and vehicle between East and West Malaysia, Labuan and Brunei.
